# Project charter: Phner Theap

Team Spicy Chicken · IT Project Management (I5, 2026-2027 Semester 1)

## 1. Purpose

Many people still make invitations by hand or send a picture in a chat. Phner Theap is a website that lets a person design an invitation from a template, share it by link or QR code, and manage the replies, in Khmer and English. The project is our final year project for the IT Project Management course.

## 2. Goal

By **22 January 2027** we deliver a working version of the platform that does the main job from start to end. What "the main job" means is written in [MVP_SCOPE.md](MVP_SCOPE.md).

## 3. Scope

What is in and out is written in [MVP_SCOPE.md](MVP_SCOPE.md). The task list is in [plan.md](../plan.md).

## 6. Schedule

## 7. How we work together

### Team confirmation (PT-001)

Before the kick-off on **26 November 2026**, every member reads this charter and confirms:

1. Your role and what you are responsible for.
2. That you can give about **5 working days a week** to the project (about 39 working days in total).
3. That you can come to the weekly planning and progress meetings.
4. Any schedule conflicts or worries, such as exams, work, travel or holidays.

Reply to the team with "I confirm" or with the changes you want, so we can finish PT-001. We keep the answers in the meeting notes or the GitHub Issue for PT-001.

### Weekly meetings

Our sprints start on Thursday and end on Wednesday, so the meetings follow that week:

| Day | Meeting | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday | Sprint planning | Check the sprint tasks, owners and anything a task needs first |
| Monday | Progress and blockers | Each member says what is done, what is blocked, and what is next |
| Wednesday | Sprint review | Show the work, check the proof, update the plan |

The exact times will be agreed by the team. Task IDs, branches, pull requests and the definition of done are in the README.
